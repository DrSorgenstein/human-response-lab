A BUILT-IN CHECK

The placebo trap

The placebo marker has no programmed causal effect. Background adversity raises both its frequency and outcome risk, creating an observational association.

04 / METHODS & LIMITS

What this can tell us.

The synthetic data help test analysis methods and expose assumptions. They cannot discover how much of humanity’s behavior is caused by these three factors.

  1. Define an outcome.This model uses a hypothetical avoidable defensive or harmful response under pressure.
  2. Specify a causal structure.Background causes, interactions, disaster exposure, and solidarity are explicit.
  3. Intervene inside the model.Changing one input while holding others fixed identifies the programmed causal effect.
  4. Test with real data.Randomize ethical interventions, follow adversity prospectively, and measure actual behavior and cooperation.
